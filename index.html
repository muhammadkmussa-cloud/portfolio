FAQ

What kind of projects do you take on?

Business and organisation websites, custom software, booking and reservation systems, POS and inventory, school and NGO platforms, dashboards, and AI or automation tools.

Can you integrate M-Pesa and WhatsApp?

Yes. Payments via M-Pesa (Daraja), WhatsApp messaging and click-to-chat ordering, SMS and email can all be built into your website or system.

How long does a project take?

A simple website can be ready in about one to two weeks. Booking and business systems usually take two to six weeks depending on complexity. You get a clear timeline with your quote.

Do you handle hosting and domain?

Yes. I can set up hosting, domain and deployment, and hand everything over in your name so you stay in control.

Do I own the system once it is finished?

Yes. Once the project is complete and paid for, the system and its code are yours.

Do you support the system after launch?

Yes. I offer ongoing support, fixes, training and improvements after launch.

Where are you based?

Kilifi, Kenya. I work with clients across Kilifi County and remotely elsewhere in Kenya.
